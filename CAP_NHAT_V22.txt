E-Reader Pro V22 - Ghi chu truc tiep va nhap bang giong noi

1. Loi da sua:
- Boi den tu/cum tu trong cung mot cau > cham "Ghi chu" se mo thang o nhap.
- Khong bat buoc to mau truoc. Ghi chu khong to mau chi co gach cham nhe de mo lai.
- Luu san vi tri vung chon truoc khi Android dong menu chon van ban.
- Dong thanh thao tac chon van ban khi mo bang ghi chu de tranh che khung nhap.

2. Micro:
- Trong khung Ghi chu, chon Tieng Viet / English, cham "Nhap bang noi".
- Cho phep micro khi trinh duyet yeu cau, noi, cham "Dung ghi", sua lai neu can,
  roi cham "Luu ghi chu".
- Nhan dang giong noi dung Web Speech API cua trinh duyet, khong can Gemini Key hay
  Google TTS Key. Mot so trinh duyet khong ho tro; khi do dung micro tren ban phim.
- Can HTTPS va thuong can ket noi Internet; am thanh co the duoc xu ly boi nha
  cung cap trinh duyet. Khong bat micro tu dong, chi khi nguoi dung cham.

3. Cap nhat GitHub / Vercel:
- Up cac file trong ZIP len repo ereader dung cau truc thu muc hien co.
- KHONG ghi de drive-config.js da chua Google OAuth Client ID.
- Sau Commit changes, mo lai ung dung, co the tai lai trang de lay ma moi.
- Du lieu ghi chu duoc luu trong sach va dong bo Drive theo co che hien co khi
  tai khoan Drive da ket noi va da dong bo thanh cong.
