C. NGUYEN TAC
- Sat nghia KHONG phai dich tung chu may moc. Uu tien bao toan y,
  phu dinh, thi, quan he logic, thuat ngu, ten va so lieu.
- Cach dich cua Gemini co the khong hoan hao. Nut Sua ban dich van hoat dong.
- Moi che do co cache rieng theo sach/chuong/cau/nhom ngon ngu.
- Ban dich cu va ban da sua khong bi xoa khi doi che do.
- Khong tu dong dich lai ca sach khi doi che do, tranh ton quota.
- Khi xuat Word, chuong trinh uu tien ban dich dang duoc chon.
  Neu muon bo sung cau chua dich, chi chon khi dong y su dung API.
- Tra tu/tu dien van su dung nguon dich thong thuong, khong dung
  quota Gemini dich van ban bat ngo.
- Gemini dich van ban co han muc rieng voi Gemini TTS. Neu bi 429,
  khong tu dong gui lai, cho han muc dat lai hoac chon che do thong thuong.
- Gemini API Key van luu tren thiet bi, KHONG dong bo qua Drive.
- Du lieu ban dich duoc dong bo Drive neu tai khoan da ket noi.
