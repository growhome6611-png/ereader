3. CAC CHE DO DOC
   Tu nhien: khong them huong dan dieu chinh.
   Am ap: warm, gentle and expressive.
   Ke chuyen: calm storytelling, unhurried and thoughtful.
   Trang trong: formal and articulate narration.
   Tuoi sang: bright, lively and energetic.
   Hoc phat am: slow, clear enunciation with careful diction.
   Cac huong dan duoc gui rieng trong speechMetadata, khong chen vao van ban sach.
   Gemini co the khong tuan thu tuyet doi moi yeu cau dieu chinh cach doc.
